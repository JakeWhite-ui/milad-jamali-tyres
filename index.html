Frequently Asked Questions

Are you really open 24/7?+
Yes — we're open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Whether it's a midnight puncture or an early-morning start, just drive in or call us on 050 394 5285.
Do I need an appointment?+
No appointment needed — walk-ins are always welcome. You can also book ahead using the form below or a quick WhatsApp message so we have everything ready for you.
Where are you located?+
We're at 47-1 Al Hajar Road, Al Quoz Third, Al Quoz, Dubai — easy to reach from all over the city. Tap the map below for directions.
What services do you offer?+
Puncture repair, brand-new tyre fitting, wheel balancing & alignment, TPMS sensor change, nitrogen air filling, new batteries, new brake pads and 24/7 roadside tyre support for cars and SUVs.
How much does a tyre repair cost?+
Our pricing is honest and transparent — it's one of the things customers mention most in reviews. Call or WhatsApp us with your car and tyre size for a quick quote.
